🧔 Developmental Milestones & Erikson

What a child can do, what they fear, and how you talk to them — the two axes that decide most paediatric questions.

⭐ Erikson — the stage decides the answer

Toddler resisting care → offer two acceptable choices. Never an open question.

“Do you want the pink cup or the blue cup?” — not “Will you take your medicine?”

🧠 What each age fears in hospital

  • Infant — separation from the parent
  • Toddler — separation; loss of routine
  • Preschooler — bodily harm and mutilation; thinks illness is punishment
  • School age — loss of control, being left out
  • Adolescent — being different; body image; separation from peers

Preschoolers have magical thinking — say plainly that nothing they did caused this, and use a plaster over any puncture, because they fear leaking out.

✅ The rules that let you work it out

  • Weight DOUBLES by 6 months, TRIPLES by 12 months
  • Posterior fontanelle closes by 2–3 months
  • Anterior fontanelle closes by 12–18 months
  • Words per phrase roughly matches age in years at 2 and 3
  • Separation anxiety peaks 10–18 months; stranger anxiety starts ~6 months

A sunken fontanelle means dehydration. A bulging fontanelle means raised intracranial pressure.

Children under-report pain to avoid injections. Behaviour and physiology count as evidence.

🧠 Preparing a child for a procedure

  • Toddler — explain just before; they have no sense of future time
  • Preschooler — a few hours before; let them handle equipment; use play
  • School age — a day or so before; give them a job to do
  • Adolescent — days ahead; involve them in decisions; respect privacy

Never do painful procedures in the child’s bed — it must stay a safe place. Use the treatment room.

🎯 NCLEX traps

  • Toddler = limited choices. Preschooler = it is not your fault
  • Parallel play is normal for toddlers, not a problem
  • Weight triples by 1 year
  • Anterior fontanelle closes 12–18 months
  • Adolescents get confidentiality
  • Prepare younger children closer to the event, not earlier
