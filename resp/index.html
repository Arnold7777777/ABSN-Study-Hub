🧠 How to use this: for ABGs, read NG-092 first — it's the method, step by step, with a decision tree you can trace with your finger. Then work NG-138 yourself before opening NG-173, which shows the filled-in path for every gas. All three agree; every practice gas on all three pages was re-derived and double-checked.

NG-194 Highest Priority Labs is the one to open before a clinical shift — it's organised by what you act on first, not alphabetically.

Two rules from this batch worth tattooing on your brain:
• ROME — Respiratory Opposite, Metabolic Equal. That one device solves most ABGs.
• PT/INR watches warfarin (antidote vitamin K). aPTT watches heparin (antidote protamine). These get reversed constantly.

⚠️ Every lab range here is a typical adult reference range and varies by laboratory — always use the range printed beside your patient's own result.
