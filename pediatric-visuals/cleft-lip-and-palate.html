🍽 Gastrointestinal · NUR 235 · Module 12

Cleft lip and palate

Visible lip gap or palatal opening; feeding seal and ear/hearing problems may occur.

← Back to Module 12Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Poor intake, choking or breathing problems needs assessment.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Incomplete fusion leaves an opening in the lip, palate or both.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess feeding, growth and airway.
  • Use the prescribed specialty feeding method and protect repairs.

💊 Treatment

  • Team-based surgical repair and feeding support.
  • Hearing, dental and speech care over time.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Use taught upright feeding and frequent burping.
  • After repair, follow exact feeding and incision-protection instructions.
