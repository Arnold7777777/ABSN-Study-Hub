Epiglottitis

Drooling, painful swallowing, tripod posture, stridor and an ill appearance.

← Back to Module 8Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

AIRWAY EMERGENCY: do not delay help for a throat inspection.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • An inflamed, swollen epiglottis can rapidly obstruct the upper airway.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Call the emergency airway team; keep the child calm and upright.
  • Do not force a throat exam or use a tongue blade.

💊 Treatment

  • Controlled airway management by an expert team.
  • IV antibiotics and supportive care after airway priorities.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Hib vaccination helps prevent Hib disease.
  • Treat drooling with breathing distress as an emergency.
