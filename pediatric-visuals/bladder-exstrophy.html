Bladder exstrophy

Visible moist bladder plate on the lower abdomen with urine drainage.

← Back to Module 13Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Drying/injured tissue, infection or poor urine drainage needs prompt review.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • The lower abdominal wall and bladder do not close normally; bladder mucosa is exposed.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Protect exposed tissue using the ordered sterile, nonadherent covering.
  • Prevent contamination; monitor urine, skin and temperature.

💊 Treatment

  • Specialist surgical reconstruction and urologic follow-up.
  • Individual continence and infection-prevention plan.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Learn skin, catheter and urinary-care instructions.
  • Support body image and long-term follow-up.
