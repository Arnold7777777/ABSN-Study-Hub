Generalized tonic-clonic seizures

Loss of awareness, tonic stiffening, clonic jerking and postictal sleepiness.

← Back to Module 7Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

A seizure lasting 5 minutes, repeated seizures without recovery or breathing difficulty is an emergency.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Abnormal electrical activity affects both sides of the brain, causing stiffening then rhythmic jerking.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Time seizure; protect from injury and position on side when feasible.
  • Do not restrain or put anything in the mouth; assess breathing.

💊 Treatment

  • Rescue medicine per seizure plan; ongoing antiseizure therapy.
  • Support ventilation if inadequate, including after medicines.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Teach rescue plan, supervision near water and medicine adherence.
  • Stay until recovered; call emergency help for prolonged/repeated seizures.

🗝 Visual mnemonic key

TIME + SIDE + SAFE SPACE. A crossed-out mouth object and restraint remind what to avoid.
