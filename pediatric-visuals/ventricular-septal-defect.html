Ventricular septal defect

Murmur; large defects may cause tachypnea, sweating with feeds and poor growth.

← Back to Module 9Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Poor perfusion or severe feeding/breathing difficulty requires prompt care.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • An opening between ventricles usually sends blood from LV to RV, increasing pulmonary flow.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess feeding endurance, weight and breathing.
  • Track fluid balance and heart-failure signs.

💊 Treatment

  • Small defects may close spontaneously.
  • Heart-failure therapy and repair when indicated.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Use the prescribed feeding/medicine plan.
  • Report poor weight gain or increased work of breathing.
