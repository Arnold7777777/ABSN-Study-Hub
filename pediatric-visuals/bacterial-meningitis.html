Bacterial meningitis

Infants: poor feeding, irritability, high-pitched cry or bulging fontanel. Older children: fever, headache, neck stiffness.

← Back to Module 7Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Suspected meningitis is an emergency; do not delay treatment for testing.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Bacterial infection inflames the meninges and CSF; sepsis and raised intracranial pressure can follow.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Prioritize airway/perfusion; appropriate isolation and frequent neurologic checks.
  • Obtain cultures when feasible without delaying antibiotics; assess LP safety.

💊 Treatment

  • Prompt prescribed IV antibiotics and supportive care.
  • Manage seizures, fluid/electrolyte problems and raised ICP.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Complete therapy and follow-up, including hearing assessment as advised.
  • Vaccination and contact prophylaxis depend on the organism.
