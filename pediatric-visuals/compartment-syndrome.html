🦴 Musculoskeletal · NUR 235 · Module 10

Compartment syndrome

Severe escalating pain, pain on passive stretch and paresthesia; pulse loss is late.

← Back to Module 10Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

TIME-CRITICAL: ischemic muscle and nerves can be permanently injured.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Pressure inside a closed muscle compartment reduces tissue perfusion.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Escalate immediately for surgical assessment.
  • Assess neurovascular status; keep limb at heart level per emergency plan.

💊 Treatment

  • Remove external constriction as directed; urgent fasciotomy when indicated.
  • Do not delay because a pulse is present.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Report pain that worsens despite medicine, numbness or tightness.
  • Do not wait for the limb to become pulseless.

A muscle in a TOO-TIGHT BOX: PAIN + STRETCH PAIN. A pulse still glowing means DO NOT WAIT.
