Gastroesophageal reflux disease

Feeding discomfort, vomiting or poor growth; ordinary infant spit-up can be normal.

← Back to Module 12Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Bilious vomiting, blood, marked lethargy or poor growth needs review.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Reflux becomes disease when it causes troublesome symptoms or complications.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess feeding volume, growth, hydration and warning signs.
  • Support individualized feeding adjustments.

💊 Treatment

  • Avoid overfeeding; appropriate smaller feeds.
  • Medicines only when clinically indicated.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Upright while awake after feeds as advised.
  • For sleep: supine on a flat safe surface; no wedges.

📘 What your course page says

Your Module 12 page also teaches the feeding measures: offer frequent feedings, thicken formula with rice cereal, use a one-way valve bottle, and hold the infant upright after feeding. For the exam, go with your course page and textbook.
