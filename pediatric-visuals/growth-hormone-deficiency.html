Growth hormone deficiency

Low height velocity and delayed bone age; short stature alone does not confirm it.

← Back to Module 11Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Headache with visual change or hip/knee pain during therapy needs prompt review.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Insufficient growth hormone slows linear growth.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Plot growth over time; support endocrine testing.
  • Monitor treatment response, glucose and injection sites.

💊 Treatment

  • Prescribed growth hormone replacement.
  • Treat associated hormone deficiencies when present.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Rotate injection sites and keep follow-up visits.
  • Report severe headache, vision changes or a new limp/hip pain.

🗝 Visual mnemonic key

A short growth ladder with a weak PITUITARY signal; a ruler measures GROWTH VELOCITY.
