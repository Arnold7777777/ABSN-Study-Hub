Otitis media

Ear pain, fever or irritability; otoscopy shows the eardrum findings.

← Back to Module 8Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Swelling behind the ear or a very ill child needs prompt assessment.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Middle-ear fluid becomes inflamed, often after an upper respiratory infection.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess pain, fever, feeding and hydration.
  • Check prescribed follow-up and hearing concerns.

💊 Treatment

  • Pain control.
  • Observation or antibiotics depends on age, severity and clinical assessment.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Finish antibiotics if prescribed.
  • Avoid smoke exposure; feed infants in a supported upright position.
