🍽 Gastrointestinal · NUR 235 · Module 12

Dehydration

Less urine/tears, dry mouth, tachycardia, lethargy and possible delayed capillary refill.

← Back to Module 12Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Poor perfusion, marked lethargy or inability to drink needs urgent care.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Fluid loss exceeds intake, reducing circulating volume and tissue perfusion.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess weight change, perfusion, intake/output and electrolytes.
  • Reassess after replacement.

💊 Treatment

  • Oral rehydration for appropriate mild/moderate cases.
  • Severe dehydration/shock: prescribed isotonic IV fluids.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Offer small frequent oral rehydration amounts as directed.
  • Continue appropriate feeding and watch wet diapers.
