Hirschsprung disease

Delayed meconium, distention, bilious vomiting or chronic constipation.

← Back to Module 12Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Suspected enterocolitis or obstruction requires urgent assessment.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Absent ganglion cells prevent a distal bowel segment from relaxing, causing functional obstruction.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess obstruction, hydration and enterocolitis signs.
  • Follow prescribed decompression and bowel-care orders.

💊 Treatment

  • Rectal biopsy confirms; surgical pull-through treats the affected segment.
  • Some children need staged ostomy care.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Learn the postoperative bowel plan.
  • Fever, distention or explosive diarrhea may indicate enterocolitis.
