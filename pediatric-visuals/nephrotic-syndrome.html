Nephrotic syndrome

Puffy eyes, generalized edema, weight gain and frothy urine.

← Back to Module 13Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Infection or possible blood clot requires prompt assessment.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • A leaky glomerular filter loses large amounts of protein; low albumin promotes edema.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Track weight, edema, skin, urine protein and intake/output.
  • Watch for infection and thrombosis.

💊 Treatment

  • Corticosteroids for steroid-sensitive disease.
  • Sodium restriction and other medicines depend on cause and fluid status.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Teach home urine-protein testing if prescribed.
  • Report fever, breathing problems or one-sided painful swelling.
