Pulmonary stenosis

Murmur; severe disease may cause cyanosis or exercise intolerance.

← Back to Module 9Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Severe newborn cyanosis or poor perfusion needs urgent care.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Narrowed pulmonary outflow obstructs blood from RV to pulmonary artery.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Assess oxygenation, perfusion and activity tolerance.
  • Monitor prescribed therapy and post-procedure circulation.

💊 Treatment

  • Balloon valvuloplasty or surgery when indicated.
  • Critical newborns may need prostaglandin.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Follow cardiology and activity instructions.
  • Report fainting, worsening cyanosis or breathlessness.
