Coarctation of the aorta

Higher arm BP, weak femoral pulses and cooler legs; critical infants may develop shock.

← Back to Module 9Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Poor lower-body perfusion or shock in a newborn needs emergency care.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • A narrowed aortic segment obstructs blood flow to the lower body.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Compare upper/lower limb pulses and pressures.
  • Monitor urine, perfusion and feeding.

💊 Treatment

  • Prostaglandin in duct-dependent newborns.
  • Surgical or catheter-based repair.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Keep lifelong BP and cardiology follow-up.
  • Report poor feeding, fainting or exercise symptoms.
