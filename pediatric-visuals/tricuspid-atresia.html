Tricuspid atresia

Cyanosis; severity depends on associated mixing and pulmonary blood flow.

← Back to Module 9Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Increasing cyanosis, lethargy or poor perfusion needs emergency evaluation.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • The tricuspid opening is absent; blood cannot flow directly from RA to RV.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Monitor oxygenation, perfusion and feeding.
  • Watch for apnea/hypotension during prescribed prostaglandin.

💊 Treatment

  • Prostaglandin if pulmonary flow is duct-dependent.
  • Staged surgical palliation.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Understand the individualized oxygen and feeding plan.
  • Keep lifelong congenital cardiology follow-up.
