Appendicitis

Pain often moves from around the umbilicus to the right lower abdomen; anorexia, nausea and fever.

← Back to Module 12Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Diffuse pain, rigid abdomen or sepsis signs may indicate perforation.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Appendiceal inflammation can progress to ischemia, perforation and peritonitis.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • NPO; assess pain, vitals and perfusion.
  • Prepare IV fluids, analgesia, antibiotics and surgical review.

💊 Treatment

  • Appendectomy or selected specialist-directed nonoperative care.
  • Treat perforation and infection promptly.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Follow postoperative wound/activity instructions.
  • Avoid laxatives, enemas and abdominal heat during suspected appendicitis.
