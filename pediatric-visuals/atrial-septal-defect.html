Atrial septal defect

Often asymptomatic; murmur and fixed split S2 may be clues.

← Back to Module 9Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

New cyanosis, fainting or marked breathing difficulty needs assessment.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • An opening between atria usually allows left-to-right blood flow.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Monitor growth, activity tolerance and respiratory symptoms.
  • Support echocardiography and follow-up.

💊 Treatment

  • Observation for selected small defects.
  • Catheter or surgical closure for significant defects.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Keep follow-up even if the child feels well.
  • Activity guidance is individualized.
