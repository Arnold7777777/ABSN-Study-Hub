Diabetic ketoacidosis

Polyuria, thirst, vomiting, abdominal pain and deep rapid breathing; breath may smell fruity.

← Back to Module 11Open the picture full sizeRead the study notes

Study notes

🚨 Priority

Worsening headache, reduced alertness or neurologic change during treatment is urgent.

⚙ Pathophysiology

  • Insulin deficiency causes ketone production and metabolic acidosis; urinary glucose losses cause dehydration.

🩺 Nursing actions

  • Monitor glucose, electrolytes, ECG, perfusion and neurologic status.
  • Strict intake/output and frequent reassessment.

💊 Treatment

  • Pediatric protocol: IV fluids, insulin and electrolyte management.
  • Dextrose may be added while insulin clears ketosis.

🏠 Patient & family education

  • Follow sick-day glucose/ketone checks.
  • Continue basal insulin and call for vomiting or persistent ketones.
