Course overview

GTPAL — COUNT PREGNANCIES, NOT BABIES

  • Twins are ONE pregnancy and ONE birth, but TWO living children. That is the bit that catches people.
What each letter counts
  • G = Gravida: every pregnancy, this one too
  • T = Term: births at 37 weeks or more
  • P = Preterm: births from 20 to 36+6
  • A = Abortions: any loss before 20 weeks
  • L = Living: children alive right now
🧠 WORKED EXAMPLE — she is pregnant now, and has had:

Her history, and which letters each one adds

  • This pregnancy, ongoing → G
  • A term daughter, born at 39 weeks → G · T · L
  • Twin boys, ONE birth, at 34 weeks → G · P · L L
  • A miscarriage at 10 weeks × → G · A

Her score

  • G (pregnancies): 4 — current+daughter+twins+loss
  • T (term births): 1 — the daughter
  • P (preterm births): 1 — the twin pregnancy — one birth
  • A (losses): 1 — the miscarriage
  • L (living children): 3 — three children are alive

💡 P is 1, not 2: the twins were one preterm BIRTH. L is 3: three children are alive.

👀 THREE LEVELS OF CERTAINTY
  • PRESUMPTIVE — what SHE feels: amenorrhea · nausea · fatigue · sore breasts · quickening
  • PROBABLE — what the EXAMINER finds: a positive test · Goodell · Chadwick · Hegar · Braxton Hicks
  • POSITIVE — from the FETUS itself: fetal heart tones · seen on ultrasound · you feel it move

💡 A positive pregnancy test is only PROBABLE — hCG also rises with a molar pregnancy and some tumors.
