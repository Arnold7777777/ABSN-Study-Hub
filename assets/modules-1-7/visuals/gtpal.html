👀 THREE LEVELS OF CERTAINTY

👀 THREE LEVELS OF CERTAINTY

PRESUMPTIVE

what SHE feels

amenorrhea · nausea · fatigue · sore breasts · quickening

PROBABLE

what the EXAMINER finds

a positive test · Goodell · Chadwick · Hegar · Braxton Hicks

POSITIVE

from the FETUS itself

fetal heart tones · seen on ultrasound · you feel it move

A positive pregnancy test is only PROBABLE — hCG also rises with a molar pregnancy and some tumors.
