Four conditions: spot the pattern

Use the pattern to guide assessment; no single symptom or lab makes the diagnosis.

Normal tests do not invalidate pain. These conditions can coexist.

Sources: NIAMS rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, gout and fibromyalgia patient resources.

RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS

RHEUMATOID ARTHRITIS

Autoimmune joint inflammation

Often symmetric small-joint swelling

Prolonged morning stiffness

RF / anti-CCP support the workup

Priority: joint function + disease control

LUPUS

LUPUS

Autoimmune, multiple organ systems

Rash, joint symptoms and fatigue

Kidney / heart / lung involvement

ANA is one part of the workup

Priority: detect organ involvement

GOUT

GOUT

Urate crystals trigger inflammation

Abrupt, intensely painful hot joint

Often the great toe; other joints too

Joint-fluid crystals support diagnosis

Priority: flare relief + urate control

FIBROMYALGIA

FIBROMYALGIA

Altered pain processing

Widespread pain, fatigue, poor sleep

No inflammatory joint damage

No specific diagnostic blood test

Priority: sleep, activity + pain care
