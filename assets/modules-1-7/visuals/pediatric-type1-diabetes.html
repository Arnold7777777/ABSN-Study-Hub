Course overview

EATING MORE AND LOSING WEIGHT AT THE SAME TIME

  • There is no insulin at all, so glucose cannot get into the cells. She is starving in a sea of sugar.
🔒 THE DOOR WITH NO KEY
  • the cell starving
  • shut — no insulin to open it
  • glucose piling up
  • So the blood sugar climbs while the cells burn fat and muscle for fuel. That is where the weight goes.
💧 THE THREE POLYS, PLUS THE FOURTH THING

POLYURIA

  • sugar drags water out through the kidneys

POLYDIPSIA

  • so she is constantly thirsty

POLYPHAGIA

  • the cells are starving, so she is ravenous

WEIGHT LOSS

  • and yet she is losing weight anyway

THE CLUE PARENTS ACTUALLY NOTICE

  • New bedwetting in a child who was dry, or a toddler soaking through nappies again. Plus tiredness, irritability, and blurred vision.

🚨 HYPOGLYCEMIA — ACT FIRST, ASK AFTER
  • In a child, a sudden change in BEHAVIOR is the sign — irritable, tearful, silly, or suddenly quiet. Check the glucose.
  • Awake: 15 g of fast sugar, wait 15 minutes, recheck. Not awake: nothing by mouth — glucagon, or IV dextrose.
  • Then a snack with protein, or it comes straight back down. Cold, clammy — needs candy. Hot, dry — sugar high.
🤒 SICK DAY RULES
  • · NEVER stop the insulin — illness raises the glucose even when she is not eating · Check glucose every 3–4 hours · Check ketones if glucose is over 240 · Fluids constantly, in small amounts · Call if vomiting, ketones, or glucose stays high
