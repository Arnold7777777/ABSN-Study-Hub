Course overview

LABOR IS MEASURED BY THE CERVIX, NOT BY THE CONTRACTIONS

  • A woman contracting hard with no cervical change is not in labor.
THE FOUR STAGES
  • FIRST: onset → 10 cm · much the longest
  • SECOND: 10 cm → the baby · pushing
  • THIRD: baby → placenta · 5–30 min
  • FOURTH: first 1–4 hours · watch for PPH
👀 THE CERVIX AT TRUE RELATIVE SIZE — 10 cm is not a metaphor

0 cm · CLOSED

  • not in labor — thick and closed

3 cm · LATENT phase, 0–5 cm

  • She is: Talkative, sociable.
  • You: Walk, eat lightly, rest.

6 cm · ACTIVE phase, 6–7 cm

  • She is: Serious, inward, focused.
  • You: Breathing, positions, often the epidural.

8 cm · TRANSITION, 8–10 cm

  • She is: Irritable, shaking, sick. "I can't do this."
  • You: Firm, calm reassurance.

10 cm · COMPLETE — push

  • Urge to push, often involuntary. Open-glottis pushing with contractions.

💡 "I can't do this any more" is the transition line — and the answer is that it is the shortest phase and the baby is nearly here.
