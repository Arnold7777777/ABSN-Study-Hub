Course overview
  • LEVEL OF CONSCIOUSNESS CHANGES FIRST — BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE
  • Before the pupils, before the vital signs, before the motor changes. If you assess one thing, assess that.
📉 THE SCALE SHE SLIDES DOWN

ALERT

  • awake oriented engaged

LETHARGIC

  • drowsy rouses easily

OBTUNDED

  • hard to rouse dulled

STUPOROUS

  • only to vigorous stimulus

COMATOSE

  • no response at all
  • ANY move to the right is a deterioration until proven otherwise. "More restless" and "harder to wake" both count.
  • Orientation goes in order: TIME first, then place, then person.

🚨 THE CUSHING PATTERN

BP

  • widening pulse pressure
  • systolic up, diastolic down
  • Pulse
  • bradycardia
