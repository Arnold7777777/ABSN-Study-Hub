Course overview

LABOR • ROM ASSESSMENT

  • When membranes rupture
  • Collect useful observations and recognize findings that need urgent review.

💡 Immediate escalation depends on the findings—not one fixed checklist interval.

  • ABSN STUDY HUB • Visual study aid • Sources and readable notes in companion guide
Rupture time
  • Step 1 · Rupture time: Record when rupture occurred and whether labor has started.
Fetal assessment
  • Step 2 · Fetal assessment: Assess fetal heart rate promptly. Abnormal findings or a suspected prolapsed cord require an immediate response.
Fluid
  • Step 3 · Fluid: Document amount, color and odor. Report concerning findings, including meconium or a foul odor.
Maternal condition
  • Step 4 · Maternal condition: Check temperature and other clinical findings. Watch for infection; follow the monitoring plan.
Plan & document
  • Step 5 · Plan & document: Follow gestational-age and facility guidance. Avoid unnecessary digital examinations after rupture.
