💧 SIADH — drowning from the inside
  • Causes: small-cell lung cancer, head injury, SSRIs.
  • Signs: headache, confusion, seizures — from low sodium.
  • Restrict fluids. Daily weights are the best measure.
  • Correct sodium slowly — fast correction damages the brain.
🏜️ DI — drying out
  • Causes: pituitary surgery, head trauma, lithium.
  • Signs: thirst that will not quit, 4 to 20 L of urine a day.
  • Desmopressin replaces the ADH. Keep water available.
  • Watch for hypotension and tachycardia — losing volume.
🚨 AFTER PITUITARY SURGERY
  • Clear drainage from the nose that tests positive for glucose is CSF — report it now.
  • No coughing, sneezing, straining or bending — and never blow the nose.
