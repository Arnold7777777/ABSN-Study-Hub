Course overview

READING THE STRIP — VEAL CHOP, DRAWN OUT

  • The shape of the dip and where it sits against the contraction is the entire question.
E — EARLY deceleration
  • Cause: Head compression
  • Against the contraction: Nadir sits directly under the peak. It mirrors it.
  • Response: Nothing. Document it.
L — LATE deceleration
  • Cause: Placental insufficiency
  • Against the contraction: Starts after the peak. Still recovering when it is over.
  • Response: Read the whole tracing · resuscitate · notify
V — VARIABLE deceleration
  • Cause: Cord compression
  • Against the contraction: Abrupt V or W. It ignores the contraction entirely.
  • Response: REPOSITION her. Amnioinfusion if repetitive.
A — ACCELERATION
  • Cause: Oxygenation is fine
  • Against the contraction: The rate rises. This is the reassuring one.
  • Response: Nothing. This is a healthy fetus.
⚠ LATE DECELERATIONS — ASSESS, THEN MATCH THE RESPONSE TO THE CAUSE
  • Reposition lateral, either side
  • IV fluid bolus per order/protocol
  • Stop or reduce oxytocin
  • Treat maternal hypotension
  • Cervical exam: prolapse? descent?
  • Notify provider early, in parallel

💡 No fixed order — often several at once. First read baseline, variability, accels/decels, category I/II/III and the trend.

  • Oxygen only for maternal hypoxia or per protocol, not routinely. Category III that does not resolve → expedited delivery.
