Course overview

THE UTERUS MUST RELAX TO PERFUSE THE PLACENTA

  • Blood reaches the baby BETWEEN contractions, not during them. Take away the rest and you take away the oxygen.
NORMAL
  • Pattern: contractions every 3 min, 60 s long
  • Strip shown: 10 minutes
  • The green stretches are the rest — that is when blood refills the placenta and reaches the baby.
TACHYSYSTOLE
  • Pattern: too often, too long, no rest tone
  • Strip shown: 10 minutes
  • Almost no green left. The uterus never fully relaxes, so the placenta never refills. The baby runs out.
🚨 STOP THE INFUSION IF
  • Contractions closer together than every 2 minutes
  • Any contraction lasting longer than 90 seconds
  • Resting tone does not return between them
  • Late decelerations, or any non-reassuring pattern
  • Suspected uterine rupture

What to do

  • Then: left side · oxygen 8–10 L · increase the maintenance IV · notify the provider.
💉 WHY IT RUNS AS A SECONDARY LINE
  • Oxytocin is a high-alert medication. It always runs on a pump, piggybacked onto a maintenance line — so it can be stopped instantly without losing the IV access you are about to need.
  • An unripe cervix is ripened first — inducing one that is not ready usually ends in cesarean.
