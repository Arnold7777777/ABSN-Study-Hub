Course overview
  • STROKE — CLOT OR BLEED, AND THE CLOCK IS THE TREATMENT
  • The two look identical at the door. A CT scan before anything else is what separates them.

🚨 THE RIGHT-SIDED STROKE IS THE FALL RISK

  • Impulsive, denies the deficit, does not see her left side. Approach from the unaffected side; bed rails up.
TWO THINGS THAT LOOK THE SAME
  • Ischemic
  • a clot blocks it
  • 87% of strokes
  • Hemorrhagic
  • it bursts
  • 13% — worse odds
  • CT scan first. Thrombolytics into a bleed are fatal.
⭐ BE FAST

B

  • Balance lost suddenly

E

  • Eyes — vision loss or double

F

  • Face droops on one side

A

  • Arm drifts down when both are raised

S

  • Speech slurred or nonsense

T

  • Time — note when she was last normal
  • Last known well is the number the whole plan hangs on.
⏱️ THE WINDOWS
  • 4.5 h
  • IV alteplase, from last known well
  • 24 h
  • mechanical thrombectomy, selected cases
  • < 25 min
  • to the CT scanner from arrival

NPO

  • until a swallow screen is passed
  • Do not treat a BP of 180 in an ischemic stroke — that pressure is perfusing the brain.
