💊 STIMULANTS — WHAT YOU MONITOR

💊 STIMULANTS — WHAT YOU MONITOR

Methylphenidate, amphetamine salts. Schedule II. Give after breakfast, last dose before 4 pm — or no sleep. Appetite suppression: weigh, and plot height every visit. Check heart rate and BP at each visit. Atomoxetine is the non-stimulant — watch mood, suicidality. Drug holidays only if the prescriber directs them.

🏫 WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

🏫 WHAT ACTUALLY HELPS

One instruction at a time. Written routine she can see. Seat near the teacher, away from windows. Break long tasks into short pieces. Praise the effort, not just the result. Medication + therapy beats either alone.
