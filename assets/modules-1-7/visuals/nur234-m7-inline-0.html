Pattern on the strip (VEAL) = what it means (CHOP)

  • Variable decel: abrupt V or W shape, any time
    = Cord compression → REPOSITION the mother
  • Early decel: mirrors the contraction exactly
    = Head compression → NORMAL. Document only
  • Accelerations: rate rises with movement
    = Oxygenation is fine → REASSURING
  • Late decel: starts AFTER the peak, lags
    = Placental insufficiency → WORST. Full intervention
