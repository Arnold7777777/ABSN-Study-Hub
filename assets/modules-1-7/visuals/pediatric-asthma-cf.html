Course overview

A SILENT CHEST IS NOT IMPROVEMENT

  • Three things narrow the airway at once, and all three are reversible. That is what separates asthma from COPD.
💨 WHAT NARROWS IT — THREE THINGS AT ONCE

The airway drawn twice: OPEN vs ASTHMA · In asthma, three things at once

  • 1 muscle spasm around it
  • 2 the wall swells inward
  • 3 mucus plugs the middle

💡 All three reverse. That is what makes it asthma.

🚨 THE SILENT CHEST

The graph, as she is getting worse →

  • how loud the wheeze is: fades to SILENT
  • how hard she is working: keeps climbing
  • The wheeze needs moving air to make a sound. When almost no air is moving, the wheeze goes — and the work does not.

💡 A child who stops wheezing but works harder, drowsy or cyanosed is heading for arrest. Escalate now.

✅ DO NOT MIX THE TWO INHALERS UP

RESCUE vs CONTROLLER

Drug

RESCUE
short-acting beta-2 — albuterol / salbutamol
CONTROLLER
inhaled corticosteroid — fluticasone, budesonide

When

RESCUE
during an attack
CONTROLLER
every day, even when well

Speed

RESCUE
works in minutes
CONTROLLER
takes weeks; prevents attacks

Watch

RESCUE
tachycardia, tremor
CONTROLLER
rinse the mouth — thrush

💡 Rescue first, then the steroid, and rinse afterwards.

🧂 CYSTIC FIBROSIS — EVERYTHING THICKENS
  • Lungs: thick mucus, infection after infection
  • Pancreas: enzymes cannot get out → they do not absorb fat
  • Gut: meconium ileus at birth; bulky, greasy, foul stools
  • Sweat: salty skin — sweat chloride over 60 confirms it
  • Growth: hungry, eating well, still not gaining

💡 Enzymes with EVERY meal and snack. Chest physio daily.
