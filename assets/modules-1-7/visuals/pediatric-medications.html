Course overview

WEIGH IN KILOGRAMS. ONLY EVER KILOGRAMS.

  • Recording pounds and treating them as kilograms is a 2.2-times overdose, and it has killed children.
⚖️ THE SAME CHILD, TWO NUMBERS
  • 20 kg — the real weight
  • 44 lb — the SAME child

💡 Chart the 44 as kilograms and every dose that follows is calculated for a 44 kg child. That is 2.2 times too much, every single time, until somebody notices.

🧠 CHECKING A DOSE
  • Doses are mg per kg — know the safe range BEFORE you draw it up, not after
  • Independent double-check by a second nurse for the high-alert ones: insulin, opioids, chemotherapy, digoxin, anticoagulants
  • If the number looks large or small, RECALCULATE before you question the child
  • Oral syringes for liquids. Never a kitchen spoon
  • Two identifiers — and ask the parent to confirm
🍬 GETTING IT INTO A SMALL CHILD
  • Never call it sweets, and never lie about the taste
  • Offer a choice about HOW, never about WHETHER
  • Small syringe into the side of the cheek, slowly — not down the middle, which makes her gag
  • A cold drink or an ice lolly first dulls the taste
  • Never mix it into a full bottle or a whole meal — if she stops halfway you have no idea what she had
  • Praise afterwards, every time, however it went
