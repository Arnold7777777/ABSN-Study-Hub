🧩 Which of the two to use

Downloaded-files playlist is the reliable one. Download the seven files from the podcasts and lectures page into one folder, put this .m3u8 in that same folder, and open it with VLC. It plays offline, which is what you want in the car anyway.

Streaming playlist opens the files straight from Google Drive without downloading. It saves you the step, but Drive puts a scan warning in front of large files, and most of these are large — so treat it as the convenient option that may skip a track, not the dependable one.

📱 Opening it

  1. Windows / Mac: double-click the .m3u8, or drag it onto VLC.
  2. Android / iPhone: put the files and the playlist in the same folder, then open the playlist from inside VLC rather than from the Files app.
  3. VLC remembers where you stopped, so you can pick a long lecture back up on the next drive.
