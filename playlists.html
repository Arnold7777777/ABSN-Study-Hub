🧩 Which of the two to use

Downloaded files is the reliable one. Download that module’s recordings from the podcasts and lectures page into one folder, put the .m3u in the same folder, and open it with VLC. It plays offline, which is what you want in the car anyway.

Stream from Drive opens the files straight from Google Drive without downloading. These lectures run from 90 MB to 580 MB, and Drive shows a scan warning instead of the video for anything that big — which is what used to break every one of these. The links now go through the download host that answers with the file itself. If one still stalls, use Downloaded files for that module.

📱 Opening it

  1. On your phone, use the orange 🎬 Open in VLC button. It hands the playlist straight to VLC by name. Tapping the plain link instead downloads the file, and Android gives it to Chrome's media viewer, which cannot read a playlist — that is the broken-image icon and the 0:00. The orange button only appears on a phone, and only on the Drive playlists, because those are the ones that work over a link.
  2. Windows / Mac: double-click the .m3u, or drag it onto VLC.
  3. Downloaded files playlists list bare filenames on purpose. Put that .m3u in the same folder as the recordings you downloaded, then open it from inside VLC. On its own, with no files beside it, it will play nothing.
  4. VLC remembers where you stopped, so you can pick a long lecture back up next drive.
