🧠 How to use this: endocrine is almost entirely mirror pairs, and the exam lives in the reversals. These pages are deliberately built as matched opposites — same layout, same diagram types, flipped findings — so you can put them side by side:

NG-284 / NG-285 hyper vs hypo thyroid · NG-282 SIADH vs DI · NG-287 Addison's vs Cushing's · NG-291 DKA vs HHS.

The one rule that saves the most marks: in primary thyroid disease TSH moves opposite to T3/T4 (hyper = TSH low; hypo = TSH high). If they move the same direction, the problem is the pituitary, not the thyroid.

Start with NG-278 if the hormones themselves are the shaky part — the three Hall of Fame pages map every gland to what it secretes and what that hormone does.
