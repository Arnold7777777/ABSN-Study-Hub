11 / 15 Medication Calculation

🔎 Open full size
✓ Reveal answer & explanation

Worked calculation

Maintenance fluid calculation

  • 7 lb 9.6 oz = 7.6 lb = 3.45 kg.
  • 100 mL/kg/day × 3.45 kg ÷ 24 = about 14.4 mL/hr. The case order rounds this to 14 mL/hr.
  • This is a maintenance calculation, not a resuscitation bolus. Follow the ordered fluid composition and reassessment plan.

Course answer / worked explanation

Indicated versus not indicated

  • Indicated: monitor SpO2; nasal suction; strict intake/output; prescribed IV fluid at 14 mL/hr; help mother pump/store breast milk.
  • Not indicated for this case: routine antibiotics, oral clear fluids while RR is 76/min, or opioids.
  • Severe tachypnea makes oral feeding unsafe; nutrition/hydration route is guided by the clinical team.
