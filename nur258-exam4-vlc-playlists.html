Open your playlist

Computer: Download the .m3u playlist below, then right-click it and choose Open with → VLC media player. On Mac, use Finder’s Open With → VLC.

Phone: Install VLC, then tap the matching Android or iPhone / iPad button. Accept the prompt to open VLC.

If the phone button does not open the full queue: Download the playlist and open/share it with VLC, or copy its link into VLC’s Network / New stream screen. Each video below also has its own VLC button.

Keep shuffle off to study in order. If playback stalls, open the same video in Drive or download that video and open it in VLC.

The .m3u and .xspf files are small lists of links; they do not download all the videos.
