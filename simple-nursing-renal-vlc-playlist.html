Open your playlist

Phone: Install VLC, then tap Open in VLC — Android or Open in VLC — iPhone / iPad below and accept the prompt to open VLC.

If the phone button does not open the whole list: tap Copy playlist link, then in VLC open Network / New stream and paste it. Each video below also has its own VLC button.

Computer: Download the .m3u playlist, then right-click it and choose Open with → VLC media player.

Keep shuffle off to study in order. If a video stalls: VLC → Settings → Advanced → Network caching → 15000.

The .m3u and .xspf files are small lists of links; they do not download the videos.
