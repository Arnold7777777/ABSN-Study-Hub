About the ranges

This game uses the subcutaneous insulin ranges from your NUR 258 Module 6 insulin table (rapid 15 min / 1–2 h / 3–4 h; regular 30–60 min / 2–4 h / 5–8 h; NPH 1–2 h / 4–12 h, often 6–8 h / 12–18 h; long-acting 1–2 h / no peak / 24 h). Rapid means injected rapid-acting; long-acting uses the general roughly 24-hour profile. Ultra-rapid, inhaled, ultra-long and premixed products are outside this four-type game.

Ranges vary by product, reference and person. They match the insulin table in your NUR 258 Module 6 materials, so clock questions here use the same numbers your exam does. These are study exercises, not instructions for insulin use or glucose monitoring.

Hypoglycemia may occur outside peak windows and with long-acting insulin. A flat peak does not mean zero risk.
