Sources & study notes

TBSA means total body surface area. The adult Rule of Nines provides an approximate burn-size estimate. This game gives exact region coverage for arithmetic practice. Its drawings are schematic; their pixel area is not a measure of body surface area.

All patients in these exercises are adults. Use age-adjusted assessment, such as a Lund–Browder chart, for children. This is a study game, not a clinical assessment or fluid-resuscitation tool.
