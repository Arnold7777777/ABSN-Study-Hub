Five letters. Two kinds of counts.

G · GravidaEvery pregnancy, including the current one.

T · TermCompleted pregnancies at 37 weeks 0 days or later.

P · PretermCompleted pregnancies at 20 weeks 0 days–36 weeks 6 days.

A · AbortionsPregnancy losses before 20 weeks 0 days. Includes spontaneous and induced losses.

L · LivingChildren who are alive now. Count each living twin or triplet.

One pregnancy stays one pregnancy, even with multiples. Stillbirths count in T or P based on gestational age. An ongoing pregnancy contributes to G only.

GP notation uses a different P: para = T + preterm P. This game uses the U.S. 20-week teaching convention; local documentation conventions may differ.
