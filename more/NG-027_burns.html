When to involve a burn center

  • Seek immediate expert assessment for full-thickness burns, suspected inhalation injury, or partial-thickness burns covering at least 10% TBSA.
  • Deep burns of the face, hands, feet, genitalia, perineum or major joints need specialist consultation. Chemical injuries, high-voltage electrical injuries and lightning also need urgent burn expertise.
  • Smaller burns may still need consultation because of pain, other injuries, medical conditions or care needs.

Read more: American Burn Association: Referral guidance

✦Check your understanding

Why can a deeply injured area be less painful? Explain why the surrounding skin can still hurt.

Reveal the explanation

Deep injury may damage sensory nerves. Mixed-depth injury and wound edges can remain painful, so pain cannot rank the burn’s severity.
