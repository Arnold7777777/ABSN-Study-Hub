CAUSE

STEP 1 · WHY IT SCARS

Years of inflammation turn a soft working liver into a rock.

🪨 Watch a liver turn to rock — chronic hepatitis, year after year

🧠 Cirrhosis? THINK “Scarrrosis.” Say it with the growl — scarrr tissue is the whole disease.

🧨 Causes — anything that causes inflammation & scarring to the liver

  • 🍺 Alcohol abuse
  • 🔥 Chronic Hepatitis — hepatitis = inflammation
  • 🫁 Cystic fibrosis — serious mucus clogs the entire body & the liver
🧠 Scars come from an A·C·He: Alcohol · Cystic fibrosis · Hepatitis (chronic) — a liver that ACHes for years turns to rock.

🧱 Pathophysiology in one line

Since normal healthy tissues get replaced with SCAR tissue, the liver becomes hard like a rock.

A rock can’t do the liver’s jobs — so every job below starts to fail.

🏭

JOBS

STEP 2 · WHAT THE LIVER DOES

4 major roles & responsibilities — know the jobs, and the failures explain themselves.

🗺️ The liver’s 4 major roles & responsibilities

🧠 A healthy liver is G.L.A.D. — Glycogen stored · Lets go of ammonia (detox) · ABCs produced · Drugs metabolized. A cirrhotic liver is NOT glad.

🚌 B — Bile: “the Bile Bus” (Memory Trick from the source)

Bile is the Bile Bus — it helps scoop up excess Cholesterol & Bilirubin and take them from the body to the toilet via the bowels.

🧠 Bile Bus passengers: Cholesterol & Bilirubin. No bus service = passengers pile up in the blood (see CRASH 💥).

💧 A — Albumin: the body’s transport truck

  • 🚚 Transports drugs in the body
  • 💦 Also attracts water
  • 🦴 Binds with Calcium (Ca)
🧠 A — Albumin · A — Attracts water, drugs & binds with calcium (source mnemonic — double-A it!)

🩸 C — Coagulation factors: the clotting crew

The liver produces coagulation factors — the crew that makes blood clot.

A rock-hard liver can’t make the crew… and that sets up the #1 danger waiting in CRASH 💥.

💥

CRASH

STEP 3 · WHEN THE LIVER FAILS

Each lost job becomes a finding you can see — or a danger you must catch.

💛 The jaundice cascade — when the Bile Bus stops running

🪨 Liver fails → no more Bile Bus service
▼
📈 Higher cholesterol & HIGH bilirubin — the passengers pile up in the blood
▼
💛 Bilirubin causes jaundice in the body
▼
👀 Yellowing of the skin & the eyes — specifically the white part of the eye, called the sclera
🧠 Bus breaks down → Bili backs up → Body turns banana-yellow. Look at the sclera first.

🚨 The #1 concern: BLEEDING

In liver disease, the blood can’t clot fast enough & leads to a HUGE risk of bleeding.

Our #1 concern is the BLEEDING!

🧠 Hard as a rock → blood won’t clot. Scarred liver = no coagulation factors = bleeder.

🎓 HESI Question HIGH-YIELD

Which complication is a patient with cirrhosis at risk for?
● Bleeding 🩸 — the liver can’t make coagulation factors.
