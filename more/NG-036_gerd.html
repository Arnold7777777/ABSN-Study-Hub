GERD 🔥

Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease

NG-036 GI ADHD-friendly visual edition

Fancy words for heartburn or acid reflux where stomach acids burn the esophagus, leading to pain, inflammation, & even CANCER called Barrett's esophagus — if the chronic acid reflux is not treated.

CAUSE

STEP 1 · THE LOOSE DOOR

One muscle explains the whole disease — when the door won't stay shut, acid climbs.

🚪 The LES is a door between the stomach & the esophagus

EXAM TIP Anything that can weaken or damage the LES — the Lower Esophageal Sphincter, the muscle that closes the opening between the stomach & esophagus.

🧠 “Loose lid = burning kid.” The LES is the lid on a jar of acid — tighten the lid (meds, small meals, gravity) and the burn stops. Everything in this whole topic is either loosening the lid or tightening it.

🔤 Say the word — it tells you the disease

  • Gastro = Stomach 🫙
  • Esophageal = Esophagus 🚪
  • Reflux = flowing back ⬆️

Gastro · Esophageal · Reflux · Disease = stomach contents flowing back into the esophagus.

🧠 Dyspepsia = “heartBURN” — the burn isn't the heart at all, it's the esophagus sitting right behind it.

⚠️ Risk factors — what loosens the lid

  • 😖 Stress
  • ⚖️ Obese — BMI over 30 (BMI >30)
  • 🫁 Hiatal hernia — at the diaphragm
🧠 “SOH” — Stress · Obese · Hiatal hernia. Say it like a sigh — “sohhh…” — the sound of a lid going slack.
🔎

CLUES

STEP 2 · SPOT IT

A burn that gets worse when they lie flat — plus the scope that looks, and the emergency that follows it.

🔥 Dyspepsia “heartBURN” — Worse pain = Lying down

🧠 “Flat = fire, upright = alright.” If the question puts the client flat after a meal, the answer is heartburn getting worse.

📈 Untreated reflux → the cancer ending

🔥 Chronic acid reflux, not treated
▼
😣 Pain & inflammation of the esophagus
▼
🎗️ CANCER — Barrett's esophagus
🧠 Barrett's = the “burnt” esophagus. Barrett and burn-it rhyme — years of burn-it turns into cancer.

🔬 Diagnostics: the scope that looks

Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy = EGD — Esophagogastroduodenoscopy.

  • E = Esophagus 🚪
  • G = Gastro (stomach) 🫙
  • D = Duodenal (duodenum) 🌀
🧠 EGD = “Every Gut Door.” The camera passes three doors in a row: Esophagus → Gastro → Duodenum.

🚨 Complication of procedures: Peritonitis PRIORITY — Report to HCP!

  • 🌡️ Fever — over 100.3°F
  • 🤲 Rebound tenderness
  • 🪵 “Rigid” or “board-like abdomen”
  • 📈 Increasing pain, tenderness
  • 😰 Restless
  • 💓 Fast HR & RR (tachycardia / tachypnea)
🧠 “BOARD” — Board-like rigid abdomen · Over 100.3°F · Agitated & restless · Rebound tenderness + Rising pain · Dashing HR & RR. A board belly after a scope = call the provider.

🎯 Cover & check — 4 rapid-fire questions
Q1: When is GERD pain the worst?
Lying down — flat = fire. Sit up after meals and elevate the HOB at night.
Q2: One muscle explains GERD — which one, and why?
The LES (Lower Esophageal Sphincter) — the muscle that closes the opening between the stomach & esophagus. Anything that weakens or damages it causes reflux.
Q3: How long after a meal before the client may lie down?
3 hours — plus small meals, sit up after eating, elevate HOB at night.
Q4: After an EGD the client has a fever of 101°F, rebound tenderness and a rigid, board-like abdomen. Now what?
Peritonitis — PRIORITY. Report to the HCP. Also watch for increasing pain/tenderness, restlessness, tachycardia & tachypnea.
