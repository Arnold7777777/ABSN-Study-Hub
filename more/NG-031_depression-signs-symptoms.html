Depression: Signs & Symptoms 🌧️

NG-031 MENTAL HEALTH ADHD-friendly visual edition

Major Depressive Disorder (MDD), also called clinical depression, is when a client experiences a severe depressed mood, loss of enjoyment in life, low energy & a few other critical signs and symptoms. Everything is low & slow — it is thought to be from low levels of neurotransmitters within the brain.

CAUSE

STEP 1 · WHY IT HAPPENS

Three neurotransmitters run low — and the whole client runs low with them.

🧠 Pathophysiology: the three tanks are low

🧠 “Sad Dogs Nap” = Serotonin · Dopamine · Norepinephrine — all three down, so the client is low & slow.

⚠️ Risk factors

  • Stressful life event ATI
  • Substance abuse disorders
  • Chronic illness
  • Genetics: Family history
  • Female
🧠 “SAD GF” — Stress · Addiction (substance abuse) · Disease (chronic illness) · Genetics · Female.
ATI QUESTION
…a risk factor for depression?
✅ Stressful life events

📝 Exam call-out: the life-event stressor

KAPLAN QUESTION
Client has recently become unemployed and reports feeling depressed. The nurse understands which statement to be true?
✅ Unemployment is a significant potential stressor
🧠 Lost job, lost house, lost person → stressor. If the stem hands you a life event, that is the risk factor.
🔎

CLUES

STEP 2 · COUNT TO FIVE

Nine symptoms exist — you only have to count five of them to land on MDD.

🔢 Diagnosis: how many symptoms? HESI · ATI

HESI ATI Diagnosis: 5 or more symptoms out of the 9.

🗣️ Anhedonia — hear it in the quote HESI

Q1: “Life just doesn’t have any joy in it anymore. Things I once did for pleasure aren’t fun.”

✅ Answer: Anhedonia

🧠 An-hedonia = “no hedonism.” No pleasure, no fun, no interest — the joy switch is off.

🛏️ The sleep complaint they test HESI

Q2: Which complaint regarding sleep would the nurse expect from a patient diagnosed with major depression?

✅ “I wake up about 4 am and cannot go back to sleep. I feel tired all the time”

🧠 4 a.m. and wide awake 🌑 — early-morning waking + tired all day is the depression sleep answer (insomnia or hypersomnia both count).

🐢 Psychomotor retardation, unpacked NCLEX TIP

🗣️ Slower speech
▼
⏱️ Slower response time
▼
🚶 Decreased movement
🧠 Low neurotransmitters → low & slow client. Speech slow, answers slow, body slow — that is psychomotor retardation.

CATCH IT

STEP 3 · WHO TO SCREEN

Kids and teens don’t look sad — they look angry, tired, and suddenly different.

🧒 Side note — Pediatrics NCLEX TIP

Adolescents 10 – 19 years

  • 😠 Angry, aggressive outbursts & vandalism / skipping class
  • 😴 Napping during day
  • ⚖️ Weight loss or gain — “suddenly” “rapidly”
  • 🙍 Low self-esteem (withdrawal)
🧠 Teens ACT OUT, adults shut DOWN. On peds items, anger + skipping class + a sudden change is depression until screened otherwise.

👤 Adult presentation vs 🧒 Adolescent presentation

👤 ADULT (the 9)🧒 ADOLESCENT 10–19
Mood 🙂↔️🙁 Depressed mood — hopeless, empty Irritability · angry, aggressive outbursts
Behavior 🎭 Psychomotor retardation — slow speech & movement Vandalism / skipping class · withdrawal, low self-esteem
Energy 🔋 Fatigue (Anergia) Anergia · napping during day
Interest 🚫🎉 Anhedonia — lost joy in life Anhedonia — quits the team / the hobby
Appetite ⚖️ Weight loss (anorexia) or gain Appetite changes — weight change “suddenly” “rapidly”
🧠 The 4 A’s of the teen 🅰️ — Anger (irritability) · Anergia · Anhedonia · Appetite changes. That is the ATI select-all, word for word.
