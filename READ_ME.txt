=====================================================================
 WHAT CHANGED
=====================================================================

 1. BIGGER TEXT
    Card text was 15.2px. It is now 17.6-18px in Verdana, which has
    taller letters and stays readable further from the screen.
    Line spacing raised from 1.6 to 1.72. Headings, tables, the flow
    boxes and the coloured bars all scaled up too.

    The "A+ Roomy" button now pushes text bigger again on top of that.

 2. EVERY KIND OF EMPHASIS LOOKS DIFFERENT
       gold bold      = the number or the rule
       mint italic    = the mechanism, the "why"
       gold highlight = the single highest-yield line
       red wavy box   = never do this
       blue chip      = a dose or a number

    Each one changes weight, background or underline as well as
    colour, so it still works if colour is hard to tell apart.
    There is a small key at the top of the page explaining them.

 3. BETTER CHUNKING
    Headings now have a coloured bar and a line under them. Cards have
    an underlined title. Table rows stripe light/dark and light up
    when you hover, so your eye does not lose the row.

 4. EIGHT NEW DIAGRAMS
    NUR 234 - umbilical cord (2 arteries, 1 vein)
            - the four stages of labour on a timeline
            - fundal height landmarks
            - VEAL CHOP as a colour-matched grid
    NUR 235 - first-year milestones on a timeline
            - vital signs by age, three columns
            - Erikson stages with what each age fears
            - dehydration severity by weight loss
