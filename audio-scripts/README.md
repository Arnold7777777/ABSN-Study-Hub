# Audio mini-lessons — source scripts for NotebookLM

Five scripts, one topic each, written to be **heard** rather than read.

## How to use one

1. Open NotebookLM and start a new notebook.
2. Upload **one** script as the only source. One script per notebook — if you
   add all five, the audio wanders across all of them.
3. Generate the Audio Overview.
4. Drop the resulting MP3 anywhere in Google Drive and tell me. I will wire a
   🎧 button into the matching study card and the quiz rationale.

## Why they are written the way they are

**No tables, no "as shown above", no bullet lists of bare numbers.** Anything
that only works on a page comes out as nonsense when spoken.

**Each one is a discrimination, not a topic.** "Diabetes" is a chapter.
"How DKA differs from HHS, and the one sign that separates them" is something
you can hold in your head at 7am. Exam questions are built on discriminations,
so that is what these rehearse.

**Numbers are spoken in full** — "one hundred and eighty", not "180" — because
a synthetic voice reads digits inconsistently.

**Each ends with a thirty-second recap** so the last thing you hear is the
thing worth keeping.

## Length

Each source is deliberately short — roughly two to three minutes of speech.
NotebookLM expands a source into conversation, so a short source gives a short
overview. If one comes out longer than about four minutes, cut the "Where this
shows up" section and regenerate.

## A note on sources

These are written from standard nursing content. They carry no citations,
because I do not have Caroline's ATI books and will not invent a chapter
number to make a script look authoritative.
