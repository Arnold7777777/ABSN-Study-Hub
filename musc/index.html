🧠 How to use this: start with NG-056 Fracture — it defines the 6 P's of neurovascular assessment that NG-058, NG-061, NG-063 and NG-174 all reuse. NG-238 Bisphosphonates has the drug-class pharmacology; NG-108 is the narrow companion on timing calcium against alendronate — read NG-238 first. NG-163 RA (autoimmune, symmetric, stiffness >30–60 min) vs. NG-168 OA (degenerative, asymmetric, stiffness <30 min) vs. NG-200 Gout (crystal, not autoimmune) are the three that get confused — read them together.
