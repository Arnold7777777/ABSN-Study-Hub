🦴 Scoliosis Treatment: Kids vs Adults

In a growing child you treat the curve, to stop it getting worse before growth ends. In an adult the spine has stopped growing, so you treat the symptoms — pain, nerve pressure, balance — and operate only when those fail.

⭐ If you read nothing else

📐 Scoliosis = a sideways curve of more than 10° on X-ray, measured as the Cobb angle.
🧒 Kids: treatment follows the curve size + growth left: watch it → brace it → fuse it.
🦺 A brace only works while the child is still growing. It stops a curve getting worse; it does not straighten it.
🧓 Adults: treatment follows the symptoms: exercise, pain relief and injections first; surgery for nerve compression, a curve that keeps growing or pain nothing else helps.
🚨 After spinal fusion: log-roll, neurovascular checks of both legs, and report new numbness, weakness or bladder/bowel changes at once.

🧒 1 · Kids: types, tests and treatment

📖 On the exam, use your textbook’s numbers. Some nursing texts start bracing at about 20° and surgery at about 40°, a little lower than the ranges above. Check the scoliosis ranges in your ATI Nursing Care of Children chapter and go with the book if a question gives a number.
🦺 Brace (TLSO, e.g. the Boston brace)
⏰
Worn most of the day and nightfor the hours prescribed (often 16–23 h), until growth stops. Out of the brace only for bathing and skin care.
👕
Cotton T-shirt underneathseamless, every day. No lotion or powder under the brace.
🔍
Check the skin dailyfor redness or breakdown over pressure points; report sores.
💚
Body imageis the big one for teens: clothes that fit over it, peer support, praise for wearing it.
⚕️ Spinal fusion
🔄
Log-rollkeep the spine straight; no twisting, bending or lifting while it fuses (6–12 months).
🦶
Neurovascular checks of both legsmovement, sensation, colour, pulses — often hourly at first.
💨
Lungs and painPCA, incentive spirometry, cough and deep breathe.
🚽
Gut and bladderlisten for bowel sounds (ileus) and watch for urinary retention.

Very young children (early-onset scoliosis) are still growing a lot, so fusion is delayed if possible. Options: casting (casts changed every 2–3 months), growing rods lengthened about every 6 months, or magnetic rods lengthened from outside the body.

🧓 2 · Adults: treat the symptoms

Two kinds of adult scoliosis. A curve that began in childhood and carried on (adolescent idiopathic, grown up), or degenerative scoliosis that starts after about 50 as discs and facet joints wear down — often with spinal stenosis pinching nerves.

Growth has stopped, so a brace can no longer change the curve. Treatment is about pain, nerve symptoms, posture and balance, and most adults never need surgery.

🩹 Conservative care
🧘
Physical therapycore strengthening, posture and stretching; low-impact exercise like walking and swimming.
💊
Pain reliefNSAIDs or other analgesics; heat.
🦺
A brace only for comfortfor short spells; it does not straighten an adult spine.
🦴
Bone healthbone density screening, calcium and vitamin D; osteoporosis makes curves and surgery worse.
⚕️ When surgery is considered
⚡
Nerve compressionleg pain, numbness or weakness from stenosis.
📈
The curve keeps growinglarge curves can keep progressing after growth stops.
⚖️
Can’t stand uprightleaning forward or to one side; balance problems.
😤
Pain nothing else helpsafter a full trial of conservative care.

After adult spinal surgery the core care is the same as for a child: log-roll, neurovascular checks, PCA and incentive spirometry. Older adults also need DVT prevention (SCDs, early walking), fall precautions and a watch for delirium; recovery is longer, and complication risk rises with age and osteoporosis.

🧠 4 · Quick check

A 13-year-old girl has a 30° curve and has not started her periods. What is the usual treatment?

A brace (TLSO). She is still growing and the curve is in the bracing range, so the brace can stop it getting worse.

What goes between the brace and the skin?

A seamless cotton T-shirt — never lotion or powder. Check the skin every day.

Will a brace straighten a 60-year-old’s degenerative curve?

No. After growth stops a brace is for short-term comfort only.

On day 1 after spinal fusion, the teen says her feet feel numb. What do you do first?

Do a full neurovascular check of both legs and report it to the surgeon right away — new numbness or weakness can mean cord or nerve compromise.

Which screening test shows a rib hump?

The Adams forward bend test. An X-ray then measures the curve (Cobb angle).
