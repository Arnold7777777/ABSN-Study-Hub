NG-311

👀 Macular Degeneration

Central vision goes, peripheral vision stays — the exact opposite of glaucoma. Dry vs wet, and what actually slows it.

🧬 What it is

The macula is the small central patch of retina that does detailed vision. Reading, faces, fine work — all macula.

So macular degeneration takes CENTRAL vision and leaves the periphery. Glaucoma does the reverse.

 Central visionPeripheral vision
Macular degenerationLOST — blur or blank spotPreserved
Open-angle glaucomaPreserved until lateLOST — tunnel vision
CataractWhole field cloudy, glare at night

Patients can still walk around a room but cannot read or recognize a face. That functional split is exactly how exam questions describe it.

👀 Dry vs wet

 DRY (atrophic)WET (exudative)
Share of cases~85–90%~10–15%
MechanismMacular cells thin and atrophy; drusen depositsNew leaky vessels grow under the retina
OnsetGradual, over yearsSudden, over days to weeks
HallmarkSlow central blurringStraight lines look wavy (metamorphopsia)
TreatmentSupplements, monitoringAnti-VEGF injections into the eye

Wavy or bent straight lines - door frames, tiles, text - is the sign of WET macular degeneration and needs urgent referral. It is treatable if caught early.

✅ The Amsler grid

A simple grid of straight lines with a central dot. The patient covers one eye, stares at the dot, and reports any wavy, missing or distorted squares.

Used daily at home — it is how conversion from dry to wet gets caught in time.

🩺 What helps

Risk factors you can change

  • Smoking — the single biggest modifiable risk; roughly doubles it
  • UV exposure — sunglasses and a hat
  • Hypertension and cardiovascular disease
  • Diet — dark leafy greens, fish; the AREDS-type supplement formulations

Unmodifiable: age over 60, family history, light-colored eyes.

🏠 Living with it

  • Magnifiers, large print, high-contrast materials
  • Bright, direct task lighting
  • Teach eccentric viewing — looking slightly to the side of an object to use healthy retina
  • Home safety: they retain peripheral vision, so mobility is usually fine, but reading medication labels is not
  • Screen for depression — loss of reading and driving is a major life change

🎯 NCLEX traps

  • Central loss, peripheral kept — opposite of glaucoma
  • Wavy lines = wet = urgent
  • Amsler grid is used daily at home, not just in clinic
  • Smoking cessation is the top teaching point
