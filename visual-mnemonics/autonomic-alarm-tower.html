Autonomic dysreflexia: alarm above the block

📖 Read the symbol key

  1. Above the block

    Sudden hypertension, headache; flushing or sweating may occur.

  2. Find the trigger

    Bladder first; also bowel and skin irritation.

  3. Act promptly

    Sit upright, loosen constriction, check BP and summon help.

One thing to keep

Treat this as an emergency. Identify triggers and follow the prescribed response plan.

🧠 Try it from memory

After a T4 injury, a client develops severe hypertension and headache. Lie flat?

Reveal the answer

No. Suspect autonomic dysreflexia: sit upright, obtain urgent help, monitor blood pressure and assess triggers, beginning with the bladder.
