Pregnancy signs: three evidence levels

Look at the objects. Name what each represents. Then try the recall question.

📖 Read the symbol key

  1. FEEL · Presumptive

    Client reports nausea, fatigue or quickening.

  2. FIND · Probable

    Examiner findings or a positive pregnancy test.

  3. FETUS · Positive

    Fetal heart activity, ultrasound visualization, or movement felt by examiner.

One thing to keep

Positive test ≠ positive sign. Evidence directly from the fetus confirms pregnancy.

🧠 Try it from memory

The client reports fetal movement. Which category?

Reveal the answer

Presumptive: quickening is the client’s subjective report. Movement palpated by the examiner is a positive sign.
