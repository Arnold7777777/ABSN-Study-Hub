📖 Read the symbol key

  1. Tank low · Hypovolemic

    Too little circulating volume.

  2. Pump weak · Cardiogenic

    The heart cannot pump effectively.

  3. Pipes wide · Distributive

    Abnormal vessel tone and blood distribution.

  4. Flow blocked · Obstructive

    A physical obstruction impairs circulation.

One thing to keep

All can reduce organ perfusion. Urgent assessment and treatment must match the cause.

🧠 Try it from memory

Why is a large fluid bolus not the answer to every shock state?

Reveal the answer

The mechanism differs. Cardiogenic shock may worsen with excess fluid; obstructive shock needs its obstruction treated. Follow the cause-directed clinical plan.
