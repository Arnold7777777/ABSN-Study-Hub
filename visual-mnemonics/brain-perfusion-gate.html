CPP: pressure in minus pressure against

Look at the objects. Name what each represents. Then try the recall question.

📖 Read the symbol key

  1. MAP · Driving pressure

    Mean arterial pressure pushes blood toward the brain.

  2. ICP · Opposing pressure

    Intracranial pressure opposes cerebral perfusion.

  3. CPP · Difference

    70 − 22 = 48 mmHg; below goal in this example.

One thing to keep

Rising ICP lowers CPP if MAP stays unchanged. Escalate new neurologic deterioration.

📘 What your course uses

Your course page gives normal CPP as above 50 mmHg, so 48 is low on your exam’s numbers too. The 60–70 mmHg figure in the answer is a guideline target for adult severe TBI — a side note, not your answer key.

🧠 Try it from memory

MAP remains 70 while ICP rises from 22 to 27. What happens to CPP?

Reveal the answer

CPP falls from 48 to 43 mmHg. Use the patient’s prescribed target; adult severe-TBI guidance generally targets 60–70 mmHg.
