📖 Read the symbol key

  1. Sensorimotor · Birth–2

    Learns through senses and actions; object permanence develops.

  2. Preoperational · 2–7

    Symbolic play; egocentric and magical thinking.

  3. Concrete operational · 7–11

    Logical thinking about concrete events; conservation.

  4. Formal operational · About 11+

    Abstract and hypothetical reasoning.

One thing to keep

Ages are approximate. Use the child’s actual abilities when explaining care.
