📖 Read the symbol key

  1. Liver detour

    Ductus venosus diverts some umbilical blood past the liver.

  2. Atrium door

    Foramen ovale: right atrium → left atrium.

  3. Artery bridge

    Ductus arteriosus: pulmonary artery → aorta.

One thing to keep

The placenta provides oxygen before birth. Lung blood flow is reduced, not zero.
