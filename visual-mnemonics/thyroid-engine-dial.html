📖 Read the symbol key

  1. LOW · Hypothyroid

    Cold intolerance, slower pulse, constipation; weight gain may occur.

  2. HIGH · Hyperthyroid

    Heat intolerance, faster pulse, frequent stools; weight loss may occur.

  3. Confirm

    Thyroid laboratory testing determines the pattern.

One thing to keep

Symptoms guide recognition; they do not establish the diagnosis alone.
