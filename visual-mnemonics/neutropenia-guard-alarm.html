📖 Read the symbol key

  1. Few guards

    Low neutrophils increase infection risk.

  2. Quiet watchtower

    Usual redness or pus may be minimal.

  3. Thermometer alarm

    Fever may be the only sign; report immediately.

  4. Clean hands

    Reduce exposure and follow the care plan.

One thing to keep

Fever during neutropenia needs urgent evaluation. Do not mask it and wait at home.

🧠 Try it from memory

Why is “no pus” not reassuring in neutropenia?

Reveal the answer

Neutrophils help produce the usual inflammatory response. Serious infection can occur with few local signs.
