🧪 Lab tests by condition

Start from the disease, not the test. Every condition below lists the test that confirms it, the ones that back it up, and the ones you keep watching — and what an abnormal result actually means.

CONFIRMS is the test that makes the diagnosis — the one an exam question is usually asking for. SUPPORTS points that way without settling it. MONITOR is what you keep checking once treatment starts.

🫀Heart & vessels9

🫀Myocardial infarction (MI)

Tests for Myocardial infarction (MI)
TestWhat the result tells you
Troponin I or TconfirmsThe one that confirms it. Rises 3–4 h after the injury, peaks 12–24 h, and stays up 10–14 days. Most specific to cardiac muscle — a raised troponin with chest pain is an MI until proven otherwise.
12-lead ECGconfirmsDone within 10 minutes of the door. ST elevation = STEMI and the cath lab is activated; ST depression or T inversion = NSTEMI.
CK-MBsupportsRises 4–6 h, back to normal in 48–72 h. Because it clears fast it is the one that catches a re-infarction while troponin is still high from the first.
MyoglobinsupportsThe earliest to rise (1–3 h) but it is in skeletal muscle too, so it can only rule out, never rule in.
BNPmonitorOrdered if the heart is failing after the infarct.

🫀Heart failure

Tests for Heart failure
TestWhat the result tells you
BNP / NT-proBNPconfirmsThe lab that separates cardiac from pulmonary shortness of breath. Under 100 makes heart failure unlikely; over 400 makes it likely. Released by stretched ventricles.
EchocardiogramconfirmsConfirms it and gives the ejection fraction — under 40% is reduced (systolic), preserved EF points to diastolic failure.
Chest X-raysupportsBig heart, pulmonary congestion, pleural effusions.
BMP and creatininemonitorWatched constantly on diuretics — potassium falls on a loop diuretic, and the kidneys take the hit if you over-diurese.
Daily weightmonitorNot a lab, but the most sensitive number you have. 2–3 lb in a day or 5 lb in a week is fluid, and it is reportable.

🚰Acute kidney injury

Tests for Acute kidney injury
TestWhat the result tells you
Serum creatinineconfirmsThe confirming number: a rise of 0.3 mg/dL in 48 hours, or one and a half times baseline.
BUN:creatinine ratiosupportsOver 20:1 says the problem is before the kidney — dehydration, blood loss, low output. Around 10:1 with a high creatinine says the kidney itself.
Urine outputconfirmsUnder 0.5 mL/kg/hr for 6 hours. Often the first thing to change.
PotassiummonitorThe one that kills. It cannot be excreted, and it climbs.
UrinalysissupportsMuddy brown casts point to acute tubular necrosis.

🧪DKA

Tests for DKA
TestWhat the result tells you
Serum glucoseconfirmsOver 250, but usually not enormous — it is the acid that is the emergency, not the sugar.
ABGconfirmspH under 7.30 with bicarbonate under 18 — metabolic acidosis. Kussmaul breathing is her blowing off CO₂ to compensate.

PotassiummonitorSerum potassium can look high while total body potassium is badly depleted. It falls fast once insulin starts — which is why you do not give insulin until potassium is at least 3.3.
