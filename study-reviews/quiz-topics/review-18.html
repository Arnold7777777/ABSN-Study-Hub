A client had a total hip replacement yesterday. Which intervention is MOST effective in preventing venous thromboembolism (VTE)?

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Elevate the affected extremity every 4 hours
  2. Encourage adequate hydration
  3. Apply elastic stockings when the client is in bed

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Early ambulation is the gold standard — it directly counters venous stasis (Virchow's triad) by promoting venous return.
