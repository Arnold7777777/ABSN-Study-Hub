Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: In postural drainage, the client assumes a position that allows gravity to facilitate the draining of secretions from all areas of the lungs. Several other positions are used in postural drainage so that the force of gravity helps move secretions from the smaller bronchial airways to the main bronchi and trachea.
