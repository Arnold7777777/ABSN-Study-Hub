Assessment: pain 8/10 in the LEFT lower quadrant, abdomen tender to palpation, temperature 101.6°F, HR 116, BP 108/64, WBC 17,800. Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)
  2. Acute appendicitis

    Appendicitis localizes to the RIGHT lower quadrant, so location alone removes it.

  3. Colon cancer

    Colon cancer develops gradually and is unlikely to present acutely with fever and a WBC of 17,800, and Crohn's would usually bring chronic diarrhea, weight loss, and recurrent symptoms.

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Keeping the bag below bladder level uses gravity to prevent reflux of contaminated urine into the bladder.
