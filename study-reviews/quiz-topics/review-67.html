A nurse is providing teaching to a client who is starting disulfiram for alcohol use disorder. Which of the following statements should the nurse include?

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. "You may have one drink per week without any reaction."
  2. "This medication will reduce your cravings for alcohol."
  3. "Take this medication only on days you plan to drink."

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Disulfiram inhibits aldehyde dehydrogenase, so any alcohol exposure, including hidden sources such as mouthwash, cough syrup, sauces, and topical aftershave, causes flushing, throbbing headache, vomiting, tachycardia, and hypotension. Disulfiram works by aversion , not craving reduction (that is naltrexone and acamprosate), and it must be taken daily; the reaction can occur up to 2 weeks after the last dose.
