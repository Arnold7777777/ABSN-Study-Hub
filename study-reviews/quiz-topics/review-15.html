A severely malnourished client began nutritional replacement 48 hours ago and now has phosphorus 1.4 mg/dL, new peripheral edema, dyspnea, and confusion. Which mechanism explains this?

Simple Nursing · Dementia vs delirium (2:47)
Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Excess protein overwhelms the kidneys, causing uremia
  2. Rapid weight gain stretches the gastric wall, causing vagal shock
  3. Vitamin toxicity from supplementation causes cerebral edema

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Refeeding syndrome occurs when carbohydrate reintroduction triggers an insulin surge that shifts phosphate, potassium, and magnesium into cells along with glucose; phosphate depletion cripples ATP production, causing cardiac failure, respiratory failure, dysrhythmias, and delirium. The distractors are not recognized mechanisms. Prevention is slow caloric advancement with daily electrolyte monitoring and repletion, plus thiamine before glucose.
