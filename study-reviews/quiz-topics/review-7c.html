  2. Aging increases hepatic first-pass metabolism, so more active drug reaches the brain

    Hepatic first-pass metabolism DECREASES with age (which raises drug levels, it does not lower them), and albumin DECREASES rather than increases.

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Older adults have proportionally MORE BODY FAT AND LESS TOTAL BODY WATER, so lipid-soluble drugs such as diazepam distribute into fat, build a reservoir, and have a markedly prolonged half-life.
