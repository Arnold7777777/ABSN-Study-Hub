A nurse calculates a Braden Scale score of 12 for a newly admitted client. Which conclusion should the nurse draw?

Why the other answers do not fit

    IT DOES NOT STAGE WOUNDS.

  2. The client is at low risk and needs only routine skin checks

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: THE BRADEN SCALE PREDICTS RISK FOR PRESSURE INJURY DEVELOPMENT; IT DOES NOT STAGE WOUNDS. Scores range from 6 to 23, and a LOWER SCORE MEANS HIGHER RISK, with a score of 9 or less indicating very high risk, so 12 signals significant risk requiring a full prevention bundle.
