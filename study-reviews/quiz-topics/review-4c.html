A COPD client in acute respiratory failure trends over 4 hours: falling O2 sat, rising RR, worsening mental status, ABGs pH 7.35 to 7.28, CO2 50 to 60, O2 70 to 58. Which trend most indicates the need for intubation?

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Decreasing PaO2
  2. Decreasing pH
  3. Increasing respiratory rate

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Rising PaCO2 is the strongest indicator: inability to clear CO2 defines respiratory failure, drives the falling pH (respiratory acidosis), and leads to CO2 narcosis (loss of consciousness, can't protect airway). Low PaO2 can often be treated with oxygen, so it's less definitive.
