Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Lithium is not metabolized ; it is cleared renally and handled like sodium in the proximal tubule, so dehydration, sodium restriction, thiazides, NSAIDs, and ACE inhibitors increase reabsorption and push levels toward toxicity.
