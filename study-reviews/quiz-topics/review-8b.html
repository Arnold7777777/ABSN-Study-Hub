A nurse is suctioning a 65-year-old client who has an endotracheal tube. Midway through the second pass, the cardiac monitor shows the heart rate dropping from 82/min to 44/min and the client becomes dusky. Which of the following actions should the nurse take first?

Why the other answers do not fit

  2. Increase the suction pressure to shorten the time the catheter is in the airway

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: NEW BRADYCARDIA DURING SUCTIONING = VAGAL STIMULATION PLUS HYPOXEMIA, so STOP, pull the catheter, and give 100% oxygen right away.
