A nurse is admitting a new client who has been admitted with a diagnosis of COPD exacerbation. How can the nurse best help the client achieve the goal of maintaining effective oxygenation?

Why the other answers do not fit

  2. Teach the client to perform airway suctioning.
  3. Assist the client in developing an appropriate exercise program.

    Supplementary oxygen is not normally delivered by simple face mask and exercise may or may not be appropriate.

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: The breathing pattern of most people with COPD is shallow, rapid, and inefficient; the more severe the disease, the more inefficient the breathing pattern.
