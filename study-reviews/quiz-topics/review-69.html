Which three Rights of Administration are INCORRECT on this order? “Give Mrs. Mann in room 705 .75 mg injection q6h” Select all that apply.

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Right Time
  2. Right Route

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: THREE RIGHTS FAIL HERE. Right MEDICATION — no drug name is written at all. Right DOSE — “.75 mg” is a NAKED DECIMAL, which is on the do-not-use list because it reads as 75 mg; it must be written 0.75 mg. Right PATIENT — a name and room number are not two acceptable identifiers, and room number is never one. Time (q6h) and route (injection) are at least stated. An order with any missing or unsafe element must be clarified with the prescriber BEFORE giving it.
