Why the other answers do not fit

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Do not argue with the delusion and do not pretend to share it. A nurse is assessing a client who has a single, round genital ulcer with a firm rolled edge and a clean base. The client reports the lesion does not hurt. Which of the following infections should the nurse suspect?

  2. Chancroid

    Chancroid gives one to three painful ragged ulcers with soft undermined edges.

  3. Human papillomavirus

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: A solitary, painless ulcer with an indurated rolled border and a clean base is the primary chancre of syphilis, and painlessness is the single most useful discriminator.
