A provider prescribes 30 mg/kg/day divided every 8 hr for a child who weighs 24 kg. How many milligrams should the nurse give per administration?

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. 720 mg

    30 mg/kg/day × 24 kg = 720 mg/day .

  2. 80 mg
  3. 360 mg

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: 30 mg/kg/day × 24 kg = 720 mg/day . Every 8 hr means 24 ÷ 8 = 3 doses , so 720 ÷ 3 = 240 mg per dose . Answering 720 mg gives the entire day's medication at once — the single most common error on a per-day order.
