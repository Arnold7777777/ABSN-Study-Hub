What is the most reliable method to confirm correct placement of a newly inserted NG tube before its first use?

Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Auscultate air injected into the tube

    Injected air can produce a sound even when the tube is misplaced. Sound is unreliable for initial confirmation.

  2. Aspirate and check gastric pH

    Aspirate pH can support bedside checks, but the question asks for the most reliable initial confirmation.

    The ability to speak does not locate the tip. The client can sometimes talk even with a misplaced tube.
