A client who drinks a liter of vodka daily is admitted after a fall. The nurse recognizes that alcohol withdrawal can be fatal because of which mechanism?

Simple Nursing · Dementia vs delirium (2:47)
Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Alcohol destroys the blood-brain barrier, allowing toxins to enter
  2. Withdrawal causes a massive serotonin surge leading to clonus and hyperthermia
  3. Alcohol permanently blocks dopamine reuptake, causing psychosis on cessation

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Alcohol is a GABA agonist and NMDA antagonist, so the brain adapts by downregulating GABA receptors and upregulating glutamate/NMDA activity; when alcohol is removed, inhibition is deficient and excitation is excessive, producing withdrawal seizures and delirium tremens that can be lethal. Benzodiazepine withdrawal is dangerous for the same reason, whereas opioid withdrawal is a noradrenergic rebound that is miserable but rarely fatal in a healthy adult. This is why the nurse anticipates scheduled or symptom-triggered benzodiazepines using CIWA scoring.
