Why the other answers do not fit
  • Tingling around the mouth with carpal spasm

    Rapid breathing lowers CO₂. Tingling around the mouth and carpal spasm fit respiratory alkalosis.

  • Kussmaul respirations with fruity-smelling breath

    Deep Kussmaul breathing is compensation for metabolic acidosis. Fruity breath points toward ketones, as in diabetic ketoacidosis.

  • Muscle cramps and a pH of 7.50 after days of vomiting

    Vomiting causes gastric acid loss. A pH of 7.50 is alkalemic and fits metabolic alkalosis in this setting.
