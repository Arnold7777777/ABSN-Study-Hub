A nurse is teaching parents about the neurobiology of autism spectrum disorder. Which statement is accurate?

Why the other answers do not fit

  2. It is caused by childhood vaccines disrupting myelin
  3. It results from acetylcholinesterase deficiency

    No single receptor mutation accounts for it, vaccines have been definitively disproven as a cause, and acetylcholinesterase deficiency is unrelated.

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Autism is associated with atypical synaptic pruning and connectivity differences , including local overconnectivity with reduced long-range integration, plus early brain overgrowth in some children.
