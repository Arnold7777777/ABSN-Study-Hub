Why the other answers do not fit
  1. Slow the transfusion and continue observing
  2. Give the prescribed antihistamine
  3. Obtain a urine specimen for analysis

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: Back pain, chills and dark urine early in a transfusion mean an acute hemolytic reaction . Stop the blood, keep the vein open with saline through new tubing , then notify and investigate. Slowing it is not enough - every further milliliter adds hemolysis.
