Why the other answers do not fit
  1. 100 mg
  2. 200 mg
  3. 400 mg

    10 kg × 40 mg = 400 mg/day .

Shared reasoning for choices without a separate note: 22 lb ÷ 2.2 = 10 kg . 10 kg × 40 mg = 400 mg/day . Every 8 hours is three doses, so 400 ÷ 3 = 133 mg per dose . The commonest error is giving the whole daily dose each time — here that would be three times too much.
