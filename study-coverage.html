Content review coverage

The required review topics are linked to their study lessons. Original book and course wording is retained beside current guidance when they differ.

NUR234: 130 listed topics across all exams and the comprehensive final. NUR235: all 17 Exam 3 items, with nine congenital defects explained separately. NUR258: all 343 listed entries across Exams 1–5 and the final; these include category headings and repeated final-review terms, not 343 separate disorders.

Graphics that would add value

NUR235: a pediatric cardiac-catheterization before/after-care diagram is the clearest remaining gap.

NUR234: useful additions would illustrate pregnancy-related cardiac failure, Rh sensitization/Rh immune globulin, Bishop score and cervical readiness, placental invasion versus vasa previa, multiple gestation/fluid-volume problems, and GBS prevention.

NUR258: useful additions would illustrate the infection chain, leukemias by cell lineage, transfusion component compatibility/reactions, emergency decontamination, and reproductive diagnostic procedures. Written lessons and comparison tables cover these concepts now.

Existing full-size graphics were reused beside their topics. The earlier disaster-triage diagram remains available for comparison with an updated adult START diagram.
