Constipation 💩

When the Gut Goes Quiet

NG-301 GI ADHD-friendly visual edition

Inability to pass stool — fancy words for: infrequent bowel movements or difficult passage of stools that persists for several weeks or longer. Defined as fewer than 3 stools/week plus hard, lumpy stools and straining.

🚽 <3/week = the ruleFewer than 3 stools/week + hard/lumpy + straining.
🧂 Fiber NEEDS fluidFiber without fluid makes constipation worse, not better.
🪜 3-step ladderLaxatives → Fluid & fiber → Change habits.
🚨 Ache ≠ surgical bellyRigid, RLQ, rebound tenderness = STOP, do not treat as simple constipation.
🧨

CAUSE

STEP 1 · WHY THE GUT SLOWS

Slow transit = more time for the colon to pull water back out of the stool.

🐌 Mechanism: slow transit = a drier, harder stool

EXAM TIP The colon's job is to reabsorb water from stool as it passes through. The slower the transit, the longer water has to be pulled out — so a sluggish colon makes a harder, drier stool that is painful and difficult to pass.

🧠 "Slow gut, dry rock." The longer stool sits, the more water the colon steals back — a sluggish colon always ends in a harder stool.

⚠️ Common causes

  • 😖 Stress
  • 🥦 Low fluid & fiber intake — not enough fruits, veggies, whole grains
  • 🛌 Immobility — bedrest, post-op, low activity
  • 💊 Medications — opioids, iron, anticholinergics, calcium-channel blockers, aluminum/calcium antacids
  • 🦋 Hypothyroidism — slows everything, including the gut
  • 🧪 Hypokalemia — low K⁺ slows smooth muscle motility
  • 🤰 Pregnancy, aging, ignoring the urge to go
🧠 "SLIM-HH" — Stress · Low fiber/fluid · Immobility · Medications (opioids!) · Hypothyroid · Hypokalemia.

🧂 The trap: fiber WITHOUT fluid backfires

Fiber inflates with fluid to scrub the GI tract and bulk the stool. Too much fiber without high fluid intake can actually cause constipation — a dry fiber mass is harder to move, not easier.

The fix is never fiber alone — it's fiber + fluid, together, every time.

🧠 Fiber is a sponge, not a broom. A dry sponge just sits there — it needs water to expand and do its job.
🔎

CLUES

STEP 2 · READ THE STOOL

The Bristol scale turns "what does the stool look like" into a number you can chart.

📊 Bristol Stool Chart — types 1–2 = constipation

Use this scale to describe & chart stool consistency objectively — type 1–2 signals constipation, type 3–4 is the goal (normal), type 5–7 signals diarrhea.

1Hard lumps“nuts”
2Lumpy log
3Cracked log
4Smooth logideal
5Soft blobs
6Mushy
7Waterydiarrhea
🧠 1–2 = plugged, 3–4 = perfect, 5–7 = flooded. Chart the number, not just "constipated" or "loose."

🚩 Signs & symptoms

  • 🚽 Passing fewer than 3 stools/week
  • 🪨 Lumpy or hard stools — Bristol type 1–2
  • 😣 Straining to have a bowel movement
  • 🎈 Abdominal bloating, distension
  • 🙁 Sensation of incomplete evacuation

⚠️ Complication: fecal impaction → encopresis

A hard mass of stool becomes stuck (fecal impaction). Liquid stool from higher up leaks around the blockage and looks like diarrhea — this is fecal incontinence (encopresis), an easy trap to mistake for the opposite problem.

🧠 "Leaky around the rock." "Diarrhea" in a chronically constipated client = check for impaction before you chart it as loose stool.

🚨 Tell it apart from an acute surgical abdomen

Simple constipation pain is diffuse, crampy, and eases after a bowel movement. A different pattern means STOP — this is not simple constipation.

💩 Simple constipation🚨 Acute surgical abdomen
PainDiffuse, crampy, comes & goesLocalized — e.g. RLQ, worsening, constant
PalpationSoft abdomenRigid / board-like + rebound tenderness
VitalsNormalFever, tachycardia, tachypnea

🧠 Never treat a rigid, feverish, rebound-tender belly with a laxative or enema — it could be an obstructed or perforated bowel.
🩺

CARE

STEP 3 · REBUILD THE HABIT

Three steps, always in this order — meds get the backlog moving, then fiber/fluid, then a rebuilt habit.

🪜 3 steps to treatment

1
💊 Laxatives & stool softeners — get things moving now
2
🥦 Fluid & fiber — the long-term fix
3
🕐 Change habits — schedule regular toilet visits after meals (gastrocolic reflex), keep a bowel diary, use a reward system for effort
🧠 NCLEX TRAP: reward the effort — sitting on schedule, trying — NOT the bowel movement itself. Rewarding only successful BMs teaches shame, not habit.

💊 Laxative & stool softener classes

ClassExample / clue
Bulk-forming 🌾Psyllium (Metamucil) — needs fluid!
Osmotic 💧PEG (MiraLAX), lactulose, milk of magnesia
Stimulant ⚡Senna, bisacodyl — short-term only
Stool softener 🧴Docusate sodium — prevention, not rescue
🧠 Softeners prevent, stimulants rescue. Give docusate before straining starts; save senna/bisacodyl for when it already has.

✅ Nonpharm teaching

  • 💧 Fluids 1.5–2 L/day (unless restricted)
  • 🥦 Fiber 25–30 g/day — increase gradually
  • 🚶 Ambulation / regular exercise
  • ⏱️ Respond to the urge — don't hold it
  • 🌀 Abdominal massage, warm fluids in the AM
🧠 "Move it, wet it, feed it." Ambulation + fluid + fiber, every single day.

❌ Never rules

🚫Chronic stimulant laxativesrisk of dependence
🩸Ignore blood in stoolreport — r/o cancer
🪵Rigid, feverish belly= NOT simple constipation
⚖️Unexplained weight lossrefer for workup
🧠 New-onset constipation with weight loss or blood, especially in an older adult, is a red flag — not routine constipation teaching.

🎯 Cover & check — 4 rapid-fire questions
Q1: How many stools/week defines constipation?
Fewer than 3/week, plus hard/lumpy stools and straining.
Q2: A client increases fiber but not fluid — what happens?
Constipation can get worse — fiber needs fluid to expand and move through the GI tract.
Q3: What's step 3 of the 3-step treatment ladder?
Change habits — scheduled toileting after meals, bowel diary, and a reward system for effort (not for each BM).
Q4: What does "diarrhea" in a chronically constipated client make you suspect?
Fecal impaction with overflow incontinence (encopresis) — liquid stool leaking around a hard impacted mass.
