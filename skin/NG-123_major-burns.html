✦2. Resuscitation with reassessment

Calculation practice versus bedside orders

When a question explicitly prescribes the classic 4 mL/kg/%TBSA Parkland formula, use that coefficient for the arithmetic. Current adult guidelines and local protocols may start differently. The first 8-hour interval is measured from injury time, with prior fluids accounted for.

🥗4. Pain, nutrition and ongoing monitoring

Labs and prevention

  • Follow measured electrolyte, renal and perfusion trends; do not expect every patient to have identical abnormalities.
  • Assess tetanus protection and use prescribed prevention for thrombosis and stress ulcers when indicated.
  • Routine systemic antibiotics for every burn are not a substitute for wound care and infection assessment. Suspected infection or sepsis requires timely treatment.

🦾5. Continue rehabilitation

Positioning, mobility and psychosocial support begin when safe and continue through recovery.
