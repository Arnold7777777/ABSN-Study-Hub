🧠 How to use this: start with NG-051 Skin — it carries the master skin cross-section and the lesion vocabulary (macule, papule, vesicle, plaque…) that every other page in the batch reuses and layers its own pathology onto.

The five burns pages are one series and are deliberately split so they don't repeat: NG-107 = why it happens (fluid shift, burn shock, inhalation injury) · NG-123 = what you actually do (airway, fluids, wound care) · NG-146 = the numbers (Rule of Nines, TBSA, Parkland) · NG-164 = rehab phase (contractures, scars, nutrition) · NG-177 = the traps, worked through.

Psoriasis vs. eczema is the classic mix-up — extensor surfaces with silvery plaques vs. flexural creases. Read NG-225 and NG-182 back to back.
