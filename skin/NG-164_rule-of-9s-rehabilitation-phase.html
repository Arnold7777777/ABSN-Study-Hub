Skin protection and life after discharge

  • Protect healed skin from ultraviolet exposure with clothing, shade and the burn team’s sunscreen guidance. Pigment may return unevenly.
  • Reduced sensation or altered sweating can change temperature tolerance. Check skin and plan breaks from heat or cold.
  • Include sleep, body image, work or school, and emotional support in the recovery plan. Ask the person which activity matters most.

Changes to report

  • New skin breakdown, drainage, spreading redness or systemic illness.
  • Increasing tightness or loss of movement.
  • A garment or splint causing numbness, color change, persistent pressure or worsening pain.
